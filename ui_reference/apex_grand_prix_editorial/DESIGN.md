## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes high-octane motorsport engineering with the elevated visual craft of luxury editorial publishing. It captures the velocity, technical precision, and aerodynamic elegance of modern Grand Prix racing, translating paddock energy into an uncompromising, immersive digital medium.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
Engineered for dedicated motorsport enthusiasts, collectors, telemetry analysts, and luxury sports lifestyle audiences. The experience should evoke:
- **Velocity and Mastery:** A visceral sense of precision engineering, sharp timing, and high-performance craft.
- **Editorial Grandeur:** Curated, magazine-tier typography paired with dramatic full-bleed driver imagery, metallic accents, and stark high-contrast typography.
- **Data Immersion:** Complex race craft—circuit telemetry, sector times, chassis data, and standings—presented with razor-sharp readability without sacrificing visual drama.

### Design Movement: High-Contrast Motorsport Editorial
The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Technological High-Contrast Darkness**. The canvas is anchored in deep carbon black, punctuated by rich espresso undertones and electrifying papaya/amber racing accents. Crisp typographic contrast, oversized track numerals, glowing sector telemetry nodes, and glassmorphic telemetry badges command attention while maintaining refined balance and generous negative space.

## Colors

The palette establishes an aggressive, high-contrast dark environment inspired by carbon composite structures, warm tire friction, and vivid paddock telemetry displays.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#ED9431` - Papaya Racing Amber):** The signature velocity hue. Reserved for high-value calls-to-action, active sector indicators, telemetry delta highlights, pole position markers, and critical energetic accents.
- **Secondary (`#45321D` - Espresso Carbon):** A deep, warm racing bronze-brown that provides rich organic grounding. Utilized in elevated container surfaces, subtle gradient overlays, and luxury backdrop tints to avoid sterile cold grays.
- **Tertiary (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp Podium White):** Pure high-luminance white dedicated to headline typography, driver names, circuit layout vectors, and high-priority textual hierarchy.
- **Neutral (`#101010` - Deep Carbon Black):** The primary canvas ground. Provides near-total light absorption, allowing white typography and luminous amber markers to leap forward with cinematic depth.

### Contextual Color Distribution
- **Backgrounds:** `#101010` base canvas, stepping up to `#191614` and `#241C16` for layered cards and panels.
- **Accents & Overlays:** `#ED9431` glow rings for apex corner markers and active driver selector rings; semi-opaque `#45321D` (30–60%) for luxurious duotone portrait masking and card gradients.
- **Text & UI Indicators:** `#FFFFFF` for primary editorial titles; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` for secondary labels, circuit specs, and metadata; `#ED9431` for active tabs and numerical stats.

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts mechanical geometric construction with refined human clarity.

### Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Headlines & Numerals (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides sharp, structural tension. Giant driver car numbers (e.g., "44", "16"), sector timings, and high-impact editorial headings utilize aggressive tracking and tight vertical leading. Uppercase transformations are applied selectively to categories, race locations, and technical abbreviations to mirror telemetry panels.
- **Body & Continuous Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean optical balance and effortless scanning against deep carbon backgrounds. Even in compact stat matrices or biographical summaries, open counters ensure maximum legibility.
- **Labels & Micro-data (`Space Grotesk` uppercase):** Used for technical metadata tags ("CHASSIS", "SECTOR 1", "TOP SPEED", "LAP RECORD"), stylized with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to echo cockpit avionics and technical pit wall boards.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model relies on a deliberate balance of rigid structural alignment and spacious editorial breathing room.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` screen margins. Supports multi-column telemetry dashboards, vertical card splits, and side-by-side driver comparisons.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins, reflowing telemetry cards into stacked dynamic duos.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Cards prioritize towering vertical aspect ratios (e.g., 9:16 or 3:4) to display full driver portraiture alongside circuit outlines.

### Spacing Philosophy
Layout rhythms favor bold shifts in scale. Cards make generous use of internal breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) around headline copy, while grouping tight telemetry badges with micro-gaps (`space-xs` to `space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through rich tonal layering, luminous ambient glows, and low-contrast ghost borders rather than generic drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Base Tier (Ground):** Solid `#101010` carbon black.
2. **Container Tier (Panels & Cards):** Deep brown-black blends (`#1A1410` transitioning to `#261C14`) with a subtle `1px` translucent border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `rgba(237, 148, 49, 0.2)`).
3. **Elevated Floating Layer (Modals & Popovers):** High-density dark glassmorphism using `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and background fills of `rgba(26, 20, 16, 0.85)`.

### Luminous Depth & Track Highlights
- **Active Sector Glow:** Glowing points on circuit silhouettes utilize multi-ring radial shadows (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(237, 148, 49, 0.6), 0 0 32px rgba(237, 148, 49, 0.3)`).
- **Driver Portrait Depth:** Gradients feather from solid `#101010` at the lower bounds into rich espresso `#45321D` midtones behind subject silhouettes, ensuring driver typography punches through with crisp clarity.

## Shapes

The design system embraces refined, sculpted geometry. A unified corner curve softens technical rigidity, mirroring the aerodynamic curves of race car bodywork.

### Corner Radius System
- **Cards & Interactive Containers:** Medium roundedness (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`) creates an elegant, tactile frame for driver photography and circuit maps.
- **Sub-cards & Telemetry Chips:** `0.5rem` (`rounded`) maintains structural compactness in tight data rows.
- **Interactive Badges & Pill Buttons:** Fully rounded pill silhouettes (`9999px`) provide contrast against rectangular layout cards.
- **Driver Avatar Selectors:** Perfect circular rings (`50%` radius) highlighted by active borders and glowing status dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#ED9431` papaya amber fill with `#101010` dark text, bold weight, rounded pill format (`rounded-full`), and subtle inset highlight. Hover induces an amber halo glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Frosted surface with `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, crisp white text, and transition to `rgba(237, 148, 49, 0.15)` on hover.
- **Icon / Telemetry Actions:** Floating circular buttons with frosted glass backgrounds (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) containing clean directional vector iconography.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact pill chips featuring a small pulsing circular node (e.g., green for Pit Exit, papaya for Sector Fast Lap) alongside uppercase `label-sm` typography.
- **Category & Flag Badges:** Subdued warm dark surfaces displaying national flag vectors and uppercase country labels in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.

### Cards
- **Driver Portrait Card:** Tall vertical canvas (9:16 ratio on mobile) showcasing high-contrast driver photography blended seamlessly into an espresso-to-carbon gradient bottom. Features prominent white driver names in `Space Grotesk`, country flags, chassis numbers, and team insignia.
- **Circuit Map Card:** Deep carbon card housing an elegant white stroke track silhouette. Key speed traps, DRS detection zones, and sector splits are demarcated with glowing `#ED9431` and white telemetry dots.
- **Telemetry Stat Card:** Minimalist square cards displaying massive oversized numerals (`display-lg`) for points, lap records, or speed traps, with micro-spaced labels pinned underneath.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Filter Bars:** Dark pill containers with background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, subtle inner shadow, and white typographic entry. Active state outlines in crisp `#ED9431`.
- **Toggle Switches & Segmented Selectors:** Recessed track containers with animated pill thumbs snapping between sessions (e.g., "FP1", "FP2", "QUALIFYING", "RACE").

### Avatar & Driver Selectors
- Stacked overlapping circular driver avatars anchored at the card footer, surrounded by a `2px` border matching the canvas, with the active driver expanded and ringed in glowing papaya amber.
