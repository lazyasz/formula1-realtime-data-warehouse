Joins: 3 Flat Dimensions • Denormalized Circuit & Dates

JOIN DIM_RACE r ON f.race_id = r.race_id

Joins: 7 Normalized Tables • Country, Engine, Nationality

SELECT f.*, cntry.country_name, eng.supplier_name

JOIN DIM_RACE_SNOWFLAKE r ON f.race_id = r.race_id
JOIN DIM_CIRCUIT_SNOWFLAKE circ ON r.circuit_id = circ.circuit_id
JOIN DIM_COUNTRY cntry ON circ.country_id = cntry.country_id
JOIN DIM_CONSTRUCTOR_SNOWFLAKE c ON f.constructor_id = c.constructor_id
JOIN DIM_ENGINE_SUPPLIER eng ON c.engine_supplier_id = eng.engine_supplier_id

DATA WAREHOUSE ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATIONS

View Full schema.md

⭐ STAR SCHEMA (PRIMARY SERVING)

  • FACT_RACE_RESULTS: 1 row per driver per Grand Prix.
  • FACT_LAP_TIMES: 1 row per driver per lap (telemetry grain).
  • DIM_RACE: Denormalized circuit length, coordinates, and lap records.
  • Indexed Slicing: Composite indices on `(race_id, driver_id, lap_number)`.

❄️ SNOWFLAKE SCHEMA (ETL / GOVERNANCE)

  • DIM_COUNTRY / DIM_NATIONALITY: Normalized geographic entities.
  • DIM_ENGINE_SUPPLIER: Power unit manufacturers and specs.
  • DIM_CIRCUIT_LAYOUT_HISTORY: SCD Type 2 tracking layout changes.
  • ETL Pipeline: Cleanses and flattens into Star schema.
