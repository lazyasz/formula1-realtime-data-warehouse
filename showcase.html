Project Description & Goals

Formula 1 represents the pinnacle of motorsports engineering and analytics, generating immense volumes of performance statistics. This data warehouse isolates and organizes complex F1 variables into structured multi-dimensional components to optimize queries for race performance, championship trajectories, pit efficiency, and qualifying outcomes.

We have implemented and documented both **Star Schema** and **Snowflake Schema** configurations to demonstrate trade-offs in relational database design:

  • Star Schema (Denormalized): Combines circuit attributes directly into the DIM_RACE table. This is preferred for performance as it reduces the number of SQL joins required to analyze race details at specific tracks.
  • Snowflake Schema (Normalized): Moves circuit attributes into an independent DIM_CIRCUIT sub-dimension referenced by DIM_RACE. This reduces data redundancy at the cost of requiring an additional join during querying.

Fact Table Grain

The grain of FACT_RACE_RESULTS represents one driver's result in one race. It acts as the central hub containing all quantitative measurements (points, lap times, pit counts, durations, standings, and outcome flags).

Dimension Tables

Star Schema dimensions represent entities providing business context: DIM_DRIVER, DIM_CONSTRUCTOR, DIM_RACE (incorporating circuits), DIM_STATUS, and DIM_DATE.
